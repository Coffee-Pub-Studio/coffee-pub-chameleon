          Roles

          What each kind of person can do. {Owners} and the admin can do everything, and that can't be turned down. {Moderator} is what someone gets in any {space} where they're marked {Moderator} on their profile's {Spaces} tab. {Guest} is everyone who comes in from {a space}'s {guest} link. Changes save as you click.

          An agent never deletes or erases anything itself. With Ask to delete ticked it may ask, and nothing goes until {an owner}, the admin or that {space}'s {moderator} approves each one. It never changes roles, makes tokens or other agents, changes this {environment}'s settings or billing, uses the host console, works outside its own {spaces}, or becomes a {moderator}, {owner} or admin.

          Kicking, muting, {guest} links and {asides} are enforced by the server. Chat, pictures, reactions and screen sharing are enforced by the page itself (they travel between browsers, not through the server), so they hold for honest use, not for someone determined to get around them.

            Layout diagnostics -- shows what this device and browser report about the screen: viewport heights, safe areas and where fixed bars land. Open it in the browser and from the home screen app, and send screenshots when the layout is wrong on a phone.
