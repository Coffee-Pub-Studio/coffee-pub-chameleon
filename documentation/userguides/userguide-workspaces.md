# Tabs and workspaces

**Audience:** anyone in a space who wants to arrange their canvas; owners, the admin and moderators also for the layouts everyone shares and for bringing everyone to a tab.

Every view of a space is a **workspace**, shown as a tab along the top, right after the space's name. The **Workspace** is the one you arrange yourself. The owner's other tabs (**Calendar**, **Map**, **Conference**, **Whiteboards**, **Documents** and **Projects**) are fixed: nobody arranges them. You can add workspaces of your own, and only you see them. Owners, the admin and moderators can also make a layout that shows up for everyone.

On a phone there are no tabs: the modules are tabs along the bottom, and the list of who is online names each person's tab.

## Who is on which tab

Small round pictures sit inside each tab after its name: up to three people, then "+2" for the rest. Your own picture always shows. A person with no photo shows their first letter on a colour. The people on the call still show in the **Who is here** list where that control is drawn. A tab folded into the **...** menu takes its pictures with it.

## Add a workspace of your own

1. Press **...** at the end of the tabs and choose **New workspace**.
2. Type a name (up to 40 characters) and save. The new tab opens and the layout picker follows ("Added <name>.").
3. Pick a shape and the module for each area, then **Save**.

You can have 10 workspaces of your own in a space. A new one starts empty, and each remembers how you arranged it. They are kept for you, so they follow you to another browser, and nobody else sees them. A guest has no workspaces of their own.

## The menu on a tab

On the **Workspace** and on any workspace you added, press the small arrow on the tab, right-click it, press Shift and F10, or press and hold on a touch screen. The menu has:

- **Open for you**: the owner's tabs that are on in this space.
- **Layouts**: the layouts everyone shares.
- **Edit layout**: change the shape and the modules. Before it places anything it asks "Place this layout?" and lists each module that will close, move or open.
- **Rename** and **Delete**, for a workspace you added.
- **Arrange last**: **Panels** and **Windows** (how modules sit on the canvas), **Clean up**, **Unstack all** (while something is stacked), **Snap to grid**, **Grid size** and **Undo**.

**Clean up** in **Panels** gives the columns one width and the modules in a stack equal heights, and opens any collapsed section. Nothing closes or changes column. In **Windows** it tiles the floating modules. Clean up and **Unstack all** can be undone: **Undo** is on the toast that appears ("Cleaned up" or "Unstacked", for 10 seconds) and in the menu, until you change something else or switch workspace.

## The shapes

**Edit layout** and **New workspace** offer nine shapes, shown as small drawings. Three are for wide monitors: **Two large, right split** and **Two large, left split** (two full-height areas and a stack of two) and **Quarters** (two columns, each a stack of two). A stack is two modules one above the other. The Calendar and Maps can't go in a stacked area.

## Manage tabs

1. Press **...** at the end of the tabs and choose **Manage tabs**.
2. Under **From the owner** are the owner's tabs. They are locked: "Set by the owner".
3. Under **Yours** are your own workspaces. Use **Move up** and **Move down** to reorder them, or **Remove** to remove one.
4. Choose **Save**. **Cancel** and Escape ask first when you changed something.

You can't rename or change the icon of the owner's tabs, or reorder them.

## Bring everyone here

Owners, the admin and moderators can send everyone to one of the fixed tabs, for example to the Whiteboards when the group should look at the same thing.

1. Open the small arrow on a fixed tab (not the Workspace) and choose **Bring everyone here**.
2. Everyone else who has the space open sees a countdown of 5 seconds with **Stay here**. When it ends they move to that tab; afterwards **Go back** returns them to where they were.
3. You can choose **Cancel** to stop it while the countdown runs.

A person who chooses **Stay here** stays. Nothing is saved: the tab is not their next visit's tab. An agent can't do this.

## Layouts for everyone

Owners, the admin and moderators make these in **Set up**, on the **Layout** tab, under **Layouts for everyone**. A layout is a shape and the module for each area; up to 10 in a space.

To share one of your own workspaces: choose **Copy one of mine**, pick the workspace from the list, and it becomes a layout for everyone with the same name. A workspace with no shape, or whose name is taken, is greyed with the reason.

## Good to know

- The Layout button, favorites and the layouts you used to save for yourself are gone. A workspace you add takes their place.
- A change is saved a moment after you make it, and again when you close the page.
- The owner decides which fixed tabs show and in what order (see [Spaces](userguide-spaces.md), "Set up").
- Stacking, windows and the other ways to arrange modules are in [The call](userguide-call.md).
